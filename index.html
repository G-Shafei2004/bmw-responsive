A Century of Engineering

BMW was founded in 1916 in Munich, Germany, originally as an aircraft engine manufacturer. Over the following century, the company became known worldwide for its "Ultimate Driving Machine" philosophy - balancing performance, comfort, and driver engagement in every model.

Today, BMW's lineup spans everything from compact sedans to fully electric vehicles, all built around the same core engineering values.
